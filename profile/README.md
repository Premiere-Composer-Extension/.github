## 🚀 Overview
Premiere Composer for Windows is an extension panel that lives inside Adobe Premiere Pro and turns hours of manual keyframing into simple drag-and-drop actions. Built by Mister Horse, the Mister Horse Premiere Composer panel gives video editors a growing library of animated text presets, transitions, and sound effects that drop straight onto the timeline. Instead of switching apps to build motion graphics, you browse, preview, and apply everything from one docked panel while you cut. It is a fast, editor-friendly way to add polish without leaving Premiere Pro.

## ✨ Features
- **Animated text presets** — Drop in titles, lower thirds, and captions that animate on and off with a single click.
- **Smooth transitions** — Apply glitch, zoom, pan, and light-leak style transitions between clips right from the panel.
- **Built-in sound effects** — Match your visual moves with a bundled collection of whooshes and impacts.
- **Free Starter Pack** — Install the extension free and get an included Starter Pack of presets to use immediately.
- **Expandable library** — Add optional content packs and Pro features when you want a larger preset collection.

## 📥 Installation
1. Click the **Download** button above to get the latest Premiere Composer installer for Windows.
2. Run the downloaded installer (the Mister Horse installer/Product Manager) and let it add the Premiere Composer extension.
3. Open Adobe Premiere Pro and find Premiere Composer under the Window menu in the Extensions list, where it opens as a dockable panel.

## 🎯 Getting Started
1. Open Adobe Premiere Pro and load the project you want to work on.
2. Go to **Window → Extensions → Premiere Composer** to open the panel and dock it beside your timeline.
3. Browse the text, transition, and sound categories, then hover to preview a preset.
4. Drag a preset onto your timeline or selected clip to apply it, then tweak the on-screen controls.
5. Save your Premiere Composer download presets as favorites so your go-to looks are one click away.

## ❓ FAQ
**Is Premiere Composer free?**
Yes — the base Premiere Composer extension is free to download and install, and it ships with a free Starter Pack of presets. Additional content packs and Pro features are available as optional paid upgrades from Mister Horse.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Premiere Composer runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit), as long as you have a supported version of Adobe Premiere Pro installed as the host application.

**Is my data secure?**
Premiere Composer works locally inside your Premiere Pro projects on your own machine. Your footage and edits stay in Premiere Pro, and you manage your Mister Horse account and licensing directly through the vendor.
